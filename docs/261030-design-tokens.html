Design tokens and shared parts

Every colour, size, corner and shadow here comes from one file, packages/tailwind-config/shared-styles.css, and every control is the real one from @repo/ui and @repo/console, drawn with the apps' own styles. The console and the site share the colours and type; each has its own shape, shown side by side.

Colours

Named by role, not by shade. Use them as bg-…, text-… and border-…: bg-brand, text-muted, border-edge. The same in both apps, except where a value for the site is shown in blue. No class names Tailwind's own palette.

Touch targets

touch-target reaches 44 × 44px around a small control, on touch screens only (pointer: coarse), so a desktop stays as dense as before. Fields grow to 44px and 16px text there too, so a phone does not zoom in.

iDashed: the area a finger can press on a phone.

Layers

What sits above what, as z-(--layer-…). Dialogs and sheets are the browser's own top layer, above all of these.

50--layer-popoverMenus, lists and tooltips opened from a control, above everything.
40--layer-staffThe staff's preview bar over the site, above its header and dock.
30--layer-chromeThe header and the phone's dock, above what floats.
20--layer-floatWhat floats over the page's content: a pinned panel, a place list.
10--layer-stickyA bar held at the top while the page scrolls.
2--layer-overlapA part that reaches over the sections after it, above their raised parts: the hero's search card and its open place list.
1--layer-raisedA part lifted over its neighbours inside a card or a map.

Still to decide

What is shared now but has a choice left open, for the next round.

  • One icon library, but some drawings are doubled: the site's car, bed and coach next to the console's car, bed and bus, and the site's person next to the console's user. Pick one of each.
  • Three pill-shaped buttons on the site are still their own: the header's, a location's “Take me here” and the “Coming soon” WhatsApp pill. A pill shape for Button would take them in.
  • The site's confirm form keeps its own Field: 15px labels, “optional” marks and the whole form's errors. Field sizes per app would let it use the shared one.
  • Step and tick discs still come in several looks across the console.
  • Spacing, widths and screen breakpoints are Tailwind's defaults; they are not tokens.
